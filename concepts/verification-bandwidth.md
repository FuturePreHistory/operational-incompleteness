# Verification Bandwidth

## Definition

Verification bandwidth refers to the institutional and technical capacity required for an observer class to access, interpret, challenge, reproduce, and audit a public claim or system output.

It includes information access, interpretability, computational resources, reproducibility capacity, institutional access, and appeal mechanisms.

---

## Core Problem

Modern societies increasingly rely on systems whose internal verification costs exceed the practical limits of most observers.

As verification becomes infrastructure-dependent, different actors gain asymmetric access to reality-validation processes.

The problem is no longer merely informational inequality, but unequal participation in reality formation and verification.
